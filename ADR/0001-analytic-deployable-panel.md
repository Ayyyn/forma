# 0001 — First benchmark: printable deployable panel

Date: October 27, 2026 (India). Status: accepted for prototype.

Evidence: the handoff's container requires closure and usable-volume verification; tubular waterbomb literature reports rigid/nonrigid transitions. Miura unit-cell kinematics are available in closed form, support an independent distance-based construction, and can be printed. Existing Crane already handles broad origami authoring, so general modeling features alone are insufficient differentiation.

Decision: implement an exact, zero-thickness Miura panel and its accordion limit. Explore grids, dimensions, and sector angle against deployed size, packed envelope, material and crease constraints. Use actual Meta tool calls to diagnose and refine candidate designs. Retain the user's warm, spacious studio direction while leading with a small exploration interface.

Consequences: no container capacity, stiffness, locking or strength claims. Physical fabrication and measured packing remain pending. The novelty target is a transparent requirement-to-experiment-to-fabrication reasoning loop and its evaluation, not a new Miura mechanism. All API preflight work remains useful. The broader continuum vision is retained.
