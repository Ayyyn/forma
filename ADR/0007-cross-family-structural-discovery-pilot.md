# 0007 — Broader structural inverse design; AI efficacy remains open

Date: October 29, 2026. Status: implemented exploratory pilot.

The user broadened the objective beyond Kresling/origami/actuators to discovering optimized structures and reducing experimental cost. Motion/programmed response is preferred; supports and instrument/device structures also matter. Requirements and physics-validity contracts should define the task rather than one mechanism family.

Implemented an isolated linear-frame adapter and graph/sizing language. Meta uses real numerical optimization, connectivity edits, computed gradients/constraint failures and geometry images. Numerical and single-pass Meta controls share the same physics, search allowance and independent verification. Four known development tasks and two seeds produce 24 cells per version.

Three versions remain separate. v1 exposed optimizer variable/sensitivity ordering; v2 corrected it and exposed provider tool encodings/list-cap limitations; v3 strictly normalizes native/XML/JSON actions, retains raw provenance, and removes an unnecessary plan-item cap while preserving the numerical allowance. No pooling or selective replacement occurred.

In v3, numerical and adaptive policies both verify 8/8 cases; single-pass verifies 6/8. Two agent graphs reduce volume against the original bounded portfolio in one seed each, but motion quality worsens, and first-feasible actual solve count is median 43 versus five. A separate post-hoc wider-catalogue check matches/improves both favorable structures. This reveals a coverage confound, not general AI superiority.

Retain the broader experimental workbench and useful structural leads. Do not claim prototype/time savings or scientific novelty. A future prospective comparison must include the broader numerical generator from the start on unseen tasks, and separately test formulation, feedback and geometry context. Other physical/device domains require matching validated models; no clinical claims are made.

All 77 workspace tests pass, v3 candidates replay and receive independent energy-formulation checks, and previous frozen sources remain unchanged. Across development there were 143 actual provider calls, estimated reported-usage cost $1.38. Physical experiments/prototypes are zero; avoided prototypes are unmeasured.
