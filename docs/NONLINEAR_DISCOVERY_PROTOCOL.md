The Princeton article points to Zang et al., PNAS 2025, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2426790122. Elastic frustration and programmable origami assemblies are established, and the paper includes global axial, global torsional and local crease prestress. We reuse the previously verified JMPS 2024 cell energy, DOI 10.1016/j.jmps.2024.105630. This assembly is an extension with explicitly ideal bilateral ring-to-ring axial/torsional springs. One-way/slack hardware in the paper is not silently modeled as bilateral or claimed reproduced. No cell/assembly invention or scientific novelty claim.

Four n=6,r=30 mm cells, each with h0 in [36,44] mm, theta0 in [28,34] degrees and two positive/two negative handedness signs. Total axial shortening fixed at 55 mm; additional end twist fixed at zero. Ideal coaxial guides exclude lateral/bending modes. Six internal DOFs remain. Search interior domains permit slight extension/untwisting; flat/contact boundaries are excluded. Find stationary points with analytic derivatives and classify by the full constrained Hessian, never appearance alone. Search is not exhaustive; absent roots are unresolved, not proof of absent states.

One spring has two externally changed rest/preload settings A/B; up to three other ring-to-ring springs have fixed preloads. Springs between non-adjacent rings couple multiple cell DOFs; geometry, chirality, topology, stiffness and preload can change. A spring spanning the entire constrained assembly is excluded as it adds no internal-state control. Axial stiffness at most 0.08 N/mm, torsional at most 30 Nmm/rad; offsets bounded in model.py. Spring routing, guide/joint design, friction, facets/material failure and contact are unsupported. Changing preload requires external intervention/work; no passive free energy or autonomous thermal response is claimed.

Task: memory-like programmable internal shape at unchanged ports. Polarization=(u0+u1-u2-u3)/55. At setting A a found minimum with P>=.35 should beat every found non-target minimum by >=.5 Nmm; at B a minimum P<=-.35 should do so. Both target and opposite localized minima must coexist at each setting. Selected constrained normalized Hessian eigenvalues >=2e-4 and selected absolute axial holding force <=1.5N. Maximize the smaller A/B found-state bias among feasible designs. Torque is reported but not constrained. Preference refers only to found minima, not a global-minimum proof. This is a synthetic research specification, not a real device requirement.
