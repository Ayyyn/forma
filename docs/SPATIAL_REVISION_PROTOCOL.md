# Prospective spatial-transmission revision screen

Frozen before generated case mechanics or scored model calls, October 29, 2026.

Question: does Muse improve verified volume or the number of numerical candidates needed to repair a design, compared with a stronger conventional numerical control? This is a small screening study, not a general or confirmatory capability claim.

The existing development case and its selected conventional fork are known examples and are supplied as the previous task/design to both methods. A fresh randomly seeded, reproducible generator creates exactly eight revisions after source freeze: material modulus, resisting stiffness, dimensions, asymmetric port placement, opposite target stroke, material+load, shape+opposite stroke and combined changes. No simulation, feasibility screening, label filtering or threshold fitting occurs in generation. Random ranges are in `spatial_revision/problem.py`. All eight outcomes remain in the report, including failures. Generated instances are untouched before this run; the problem family and distributions are developer-designed and familiar, so these are not independent real engineering briefs.

Each method has 256 charged candidate slots per instance and the same starting design, exact current constraints, circular spatial frame model, ground graph, analytic sensitivities and constrained sizing optimizer. The first new response of the previous design consumes a common slot. Identical cached candidates cost no additional slot and do not reassemble mechanics. Every optimizer sample consumes a slot; invalid new candidates/tool decisions consume one. Numerical eligibility/collision checks alone use no mechanics and may be performed without a simulation charge. Every valid candidate's full guards decide acceptance. The optimizer directly constrains signed output stroke, input travel and parasitic x/y, while other guards can reject candidates. Summed volume is an idealized proxy including joint overlap.

Numerical control: warm-start repair for positive target-signed motion, up to 80 seeded geometrically eligible conventional catalogue screens, sizing on three promising distinct graphs, then member removal/sizing within remaining allowance. The expanded conventional catalogue includes all anchor-pair/ordered side-node forks and braced variants. All eligible graphs are also shown to Muse. This is a bounded strong control for this graph language, not a global optimizer or best possible algorithm guarantee.

Muse: `muse-wonder-1.0`, temperature 0.2, medium reasoning, at most four real requests and 6144 output tokens/request; no HTTP retries/provider fallback. Tool evaluation, optimization, cached multi-view geometry/deformation inspection and numerical sensitivities are available. Received native/XML public tool commands are strictly normalized using the tested adapter; all received public output and failures remain recorded. No model is trained. No cross-case numerical answers/history are provided. One stochastic trajectory per instance; seed 101 controls numerical sampling, and remote output determinism is not promised. Single-pass, image/no-image, agent-team and model comparisons are not tested in this matrix.

Primary outcomes: externally verified feasibility/completion; quality among jointly feasible pairs; first-feasible candidate count for initially failing cases. Also report all actual assemblies, invalid/cached work, wall time, real provider calls/usage and estimated cost. No physical prototypes or prototype savings are inferred. Different methods can stop early; a lower used allowance alone is not evidence of efficiency. A previously feasible starter remains an explicit initial-feasible outcome; no model credit for discovering it.

Predeclared screening signal: Muse must have at least as many verified feasible cases as numerical control AND either (a) median paired volume reduction at least 10%, positive lower bound of a 95% case-bootstrap interval and at least six jointly feasible cases, OR (b) median first-feasible query reduction at least 25%, positive lower interval bound, at least six initially failing jointly feasible cases, and no more than 5% median final-volume worsening. Use 10,000 bootstrap replicates, RNG seed 10292026. Positive signals still need repeated trajectories and independent tasks. Failure to pass means no evidence sufficient for this particular gate, not proof all AI assistance is ineffective.

Before this study the six-DOF frame adapter passed analytical/quadrature checks and comparison with OpenSees 3.8.0 over 272 cases (all 254 previous candidates plus 18 separate direction/orientation cases). Source hashes and external-check receipt are frozen. After the matrix, every valid new candidate is replayed through production plus independent energy assembly offline, and every selected result is checked with external OpenSees under identical assumptions. Extra audit solves are recorded and unavailable to search. Do not change a frozen source or replace failures; integration corrections require a separately labeled study version.

Physics: infinitesimal linear Euler–Bernoulli beams with circular sections, torsion, rigid joints, clamps, external force and grounded resisting spring; synthetic isotropic coefficients. No shear deformation, finite-displacement/contact/fatigue/joint concentration/material calibration or loaded-system buckling certification. Initial member-envelope checks exclude intentional joint overlap. Rotations are sampled at 17 sections/member. Nominal strain and pinned-member compression ratios are guards, not structural safety certificates. Negative target z uses a sign convention, not negative spring stiffness. Energy stored/input ramp work is not cyclic efficiency. No medical advice, clinical performance or physical-validation claim.

OpenSees is separately installed for local research validation only, excluded from source snapshots and Git. Installation receipt/binary hash and upstream licensing are retained locally; there is no redistribution or deployed service using that dependency. Attribution: [OpenSees elastic beam documentation](https://openseespydoc.readthedocs.io/en/latest/src/elasticBeamColumn.html), [linear transformation](https://opensees.github.io/OpenSeesDocumentation/user/manual/model/geomTransf/Linear.html), [classical loaded compliant-mechanism example](https://pymoto.readthedocs.io/en/latest/auto_examples/topology_optimization/ex_compliant_mechanism_springs.html). New work is integration/evaluation, not the invention of compliant transmissions, inverse design or topology optimization.
