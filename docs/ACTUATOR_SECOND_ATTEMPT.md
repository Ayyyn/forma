# A better second experiment: Meta with mechanics and geometry tools

Date: October 29, 2026. Status: researched proposal plus an unscored offline assembly probe. **No new Meta calls, no new scored AI benchmark and no hardware test were made for this reassessment.**

## Recommendation

Give a substantially better equipped actuator-design agent one bounded second attempt. The previous experiment-selection result stands. It did not test an agent with numerical computation, optimization, geometric inspection or authority to compose and revise a mechanism. Extending that result into a general dismissal of origami actuator design was too broad.

The scientific question is whether Meta can translate a motion requirement into a mechanically checked actuator design, select and compose allowed modules, use numerical tools, diagnose failures, and repair a design while preserving requirements. More tools make this test possible; they do not establish an advantage by themselves. Numerical optimizers should be tools available to the agent and strong independent controls.

The recommended first scope is a **guided coaxial modular Kresling actuator**, driven by an ideal imposed axial stroke, with end rotation free. Design for requested axial stroke, additional end twist, envelope, reaction-force ceiling and local constrained stability. Consider two to four cells, handedness, cell geometry and declared ideal parallel axial springs. This is a small reusable mechanical design language, not arbitrary CAD or a validated general robotic arm. A motor, cable path, guide, joints and actual parts are later hardware questions; the first study represents their global stroke as an ideal constraint.

An illustrative request is: “Shorten 20 mm, keep additional tool rotation within 5 degrees through the stroke, stay inside the specified envelope and pulling-force budget, and flag mechanically unstable configurations.” The 20 mm/5 degree numbers are proposed synthetic test requirements, not measured customer demand or a claimed achieved actuator specification.

## Why the old interface limited the question

The actual old schema in `kresling_bench/policies.py` exposes exactly one function: `perform_experiment(experiment_id, public_reason)`. The model receives a sizeable numeric response table and Bayesian histories, then selects a listed ID. It gets useful statistical context, but has no calculator/Python execution, optimizer, geometry renderer, editable design, or tool for comparing alternative mechanical hypotheses it creates. The deterministic baseline numerically integrates information gain; Meta has to approximate a choice from its input context.

This was a valid comparison of that fixed selector and its resource limit. It was not an upper bound on a numerical-tool-using Meta system. Eight output-limit failures also justify examining context compression and execution tools as reliability interventions, rather than just raising token limits. The old result must not be erased, pooled with a later revision or relabeled as a successful agent test.

## Actuator research and feasibility

1. **Geometry-programmed tendon actuation.** Polamraju and Pratapa, [Geometry-driven programmable actuation of a Kresling origami soft robotic arm](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmecsci.2026.111835), IJMS 326 (2026), reports geometry-selected actuation sequences from a global tendon input, with a physical modular demonstration and comparisons of geometric/energy frameworks. This is particularly relevant prior art: sequencing modules by geometry is established, not our invention. We inspected the indexed abstract/highlights; the direct publisher page returned HTTP 403, so its full equations, source code and dataset have **not** been verified. Its bending/sequence predictions cannot simply be claimed as reproduced by our two-coordinate cell model.
2. **Pneumatic nonlinear modules.** Oliveira et al., [Meta-Ori](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.23375v1), RoboSoft 2025, describes TPU-printed Kresling inflatables combined with nonlinear metashells and a two-segment sequencing demonstration. The lab links a [Grasshopper generator](https://github.com/hdsoliveira/Meta-Ori), whose repository displays GPL-3.0. This is useful prior art and a possible geometry reference. Its pressure/volume and shell response require additional modeling and fabrication work; our existing spring/bar/hinge energy is not a validated pneumatic model. No repository code was copied or run.
3. **Composable folding motion primitives.** Jang et al., [One Print, Many Moves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00751), a September 2026 preprint, describes monolithic modules composed into multiple motion functions. We inspected its abstract; no public executable simulator or dataset was verified. This supports composition as a research direction, but not an assumption that we can reproduce its material behavior within days.
4. **Sensor-based control.** Mensah et al., [Hold 'em and Fold 'em](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04650v2), 2024, provides a real example of feedback-controlled pneumatic origami and links CAD/ROS resources. The project-site fetch failed in this session. It helps distinguish a demonstrated actuator/control system from our present numerical model, but would require a different experimental setup.
5. **Electrothermal actuation.** Wu et al., [Modular multi-degree-of-freedom soft origami robots with reprogrammable electrothermal actuation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11098090/), PNAS 2024, is a relevant physical platform. Its specialized heaters/materials and multiphysics are a larger extension of our current validated model, so it is not the recommended first implementation.

Prior AI design also exists: [Zhu and Filipov, interpretable origami inverse design](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-23875-6), with [TreeForOrigami](https://github.com/zzhuyii/TreeForOrigami), covers categorical/continuous design and actuator-related objectives. Kang et al.'s [2025 PINN inverse-design work](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5MH01607J) was already reviewed in the proof. [COrigami](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26299) studies natural-language-driven visual origami. These are not equivalent to the proposed mechanics-checked actuator workflow, but rule out broad “first AI origami design” claims. Public visibility of code does not settle reuse permissions; no third-party source/data is being included here.

My feasibility judgment: start with the existing, source-checked mechanical energy and a constrained assembly, so the main new engineering is the tool interface and orchestration. A general pneumatic/electrothermal multi-DoF robot would require too much new unverified physics. Full arbitrary topology creation is also outside the first credible test.

## Concrete offline probe completed today

Run `python research/actuator_assembly_probe.py`. Results and a scientific stability plot are in `research/actuator-assembly-probe/`.

The probe uses two identical published-reference cells in series. Total shortening is imposed; each cell's shortening and local twist can adjust. The internal ring stays coaxial under an ideal guide. Reflected cells have opposite physical twist signs while retaining the same local energy. Rest orientation is subtracted when reporting additional end twist.

At 20 mm total shortening, the symmetric state has 10 mm shortening and 28.633416 degrees local twist per cell. Opposite-handed cells cancel additional end twist; same-handed cells produce 57.266832 degrees. That geometry alone does not certify the assembly.

| Symmetric assembly at 20 mm imposed stroke | Bare cells | Ideal spring 0.25 N/mm per cell |
|---|---:|---:|
| Additional opposite-handed end twist | 0 degrees | 0 degrees |
| Axial reaction | 0.315162 N | 2.815162 N |
| Smallest dimensionless constrained Hessian eigenvalue | -0.055103 | +0.024666 |
| Local classification within modeled coordinates | Unstable | Strict local minimum |

For free coordinates x=(u1/r, phi1, phi2), enforce u2=Utotal-u1 and calculate the full projected Hessian of the summed cell energies. Independent vertex/face-normal energy finite differences agree with the analytic projected Hessian within 1.27e-8 at a dimensionless step of 1e-4. Perturbing along the softest mode lowers the bare energy and raises the stabilized energy, confirming the sign distinction. Constrained stationarity residual is below 4.2e-16.

The stabilized symmetric branch has positive constrained eigenvalues at all 21 sampled total strokes from 0 to 20 mm; the smallest sampled value is 0.013805. This is **sampled local stability**, not continuous-path certification, an exhaustive equilibrium search, a global minimum, dynamic switching verification or a hardware result. The ideal springs are declared numerical components, not calibrated physical parts. No claim is made about fabrication tolerances, off-axis modes, connector stiffness, tendon routing/friction, contact or payload dynamics.

The probe is deliberately an unscored development diagnostic. I selected the repair using mechanical analysis; **Meta did not generate it**. It shows a useful testbed failure and a force/stability tradeoff, not AI value or a new mechanical discovery. It must be excluded from a later held-out evaluation.

## Proposed tool architecture

Keep an authoritative typed design state: cell parameters, handedness, topology, coordinate frames, units, load-control protocol, solver settings, requirement versions and source/model-validity notes. Meta edits a bounded design program; it never overwrites the requirements or verification verdict.

| Tool | Returned evidence / useful capability |
|---|---|
| `inspect_model` | Equations, coordinate conventions, supported physics and source references; prevents substituting an incompatible model. |
| `inspect_geometry` | Exact module graph, dimensions and coordinate frames; optional generated multi-view meshes/images with numerical labels. Render the actual design state. |
| `analyze_branch` | Equilibrium residuals, projected Hessian eigenvalues, stroke/twist/force curves, failed solves and boundary flags. |
| `optimize_design` | A real bounded numerical optimizer for continuous parameters within a selected topology; return feasible/Pareto candidates and charged evaluations. |
| `compare_or_perturb` | Counterfactual design comparison and numerical sensitivity to declared parameter perturbations; not assumed manufacturing statistics. |
| `verify_design` | Independent energy/derivative checks, all hard-constraint margins and provenance-linked result artifacts. |

Keep compact summaries and stable artifact IDs in context; retrieve detailed numeric tables or views on demand. Preserve proper assistant tool-call/result history. Separate deliberation from numerical execution. Failed tool calls and invalid proposals retain explicit statuses; reserve completion capacity and report a failure instead of claiming success without verification.

Numerical state is primary for dimensions and force/stability. Rendered views can help when topology, handedness, placement or envelope reasoning is relevant. They are an experimental input modality, not a substitute for the equations or a guaranteed improvement. An image generator is not a mechanical geometry engine.

## Fair second evaluation

Two questions should be kept separate:

**Search/orchestration on a supplied exact specification:** compare (a) enumeration of the allowed topology/handedness grammar plus a well-tuned continuous numerical optimizer, (b) Meta with the same optimizer and analysis tools, and (c) simpler Meta planning with numerical execution. Supply the same constraints and physics, use equal charged model-evaluation allowances, and report additional model tokens/time/money separately. If the numerical pipeline solves the same tasks more simply, report that.

**End-to-end design from a brief:** both the fixed pipeline and adaptive pipeline should receive the same Meta brief-to-spec compiler or another demonstrably comparable interface. Otherwise “the numeric baseline cannot read English” would manufacture an AI advantage. Independently check unit conversion, preservation of hard constraints and implied motion requirements. Measure whether tool feedback improves verified completion and reduces specification/engineering errors; do not infer human time saved without observing it.

Ablations should isolate numerical-tool access, compact geometric state and optional rendered views. Both policies must access the same scene/constraints; adding images only to one system cannot support a claim about pure search quality without disclosing the information difference. Every optimizer sample, derivative/robustness evaluation and retry needs consistent accounting. Count internal work within optimizer calls, not just the number of high-level tools.

Use new held-out briefs and design targets across cancellation, desired rotation, competing force/envelope limits, incompatible/unsupported requests and controlled revisions. A simple known opposite-handed pair or today's spring repair is a development example, not a headline result. Provide a fixed-template control too. Preserve all methods, seeds, failures and source hashes; separate small unscored tool-reliability diagnostics from the frozen scored batch. Pick new success criteria from the actual capability and practical effect required before that batch. Do not reuse the old 1.48% result as evidence for this different system.

## Scope and next decision

I recommend one bounded development cycle to build and validate the assembly evaluator and these few tools, then a small real-Meta diagnostic. Only after the tool interface is reliable should we freeze and spend on the new comparison. This document is not that freeze. No full paid rerun is warranted merely to change the prompt or increase the token limit.

Continue only on measured capability: correctly specified, independently checked designs/repairs on unseen tasks, meaningful improvement over the numerical pipeline or the one-pass compiler baseline, and a demo that clearly shows what the model contributes. The anticipated contribution is an auditable Meta design workflow with evidence of actuator-level usefulness. It is not invention of origami actuators, chirality cancellation, multistability, inverse design or numerical optimization.

The [official Push the Frontier theme](https://dev.meta.ai/events/global-hackathon) explicitly includes robotics, manufacturing and better understanding/control of physical systems. That supports a capable design tool as a theme fit; it does not guarantee competitive judging. A measured physical demonstration would strengthen the evidence, but physical work remains deferred at the user's request. The first proposed deliverable must therefore be presented honestly as a computational research prototype. Keep new work and reused literature/software attribution clear for the November 4 submission.
